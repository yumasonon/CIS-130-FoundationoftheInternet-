About Me

It all started in Southern California, San Bernardino County.

I was a punk millenial kid who though they knew what the world was about. Well life had other plans.

I thought blue collar was the way even though I had on multiple occasions attempted to teach myself how to code.

Blue collar was fun, but wasn't where my heart was. I knew the pasture was greener.

So I needed some easy way to get my foot in the door to Information Tech and I figured signing my life away was the ticket.

It was.

So after going through the military and fast-forward a few years, I know am on the path I always wanted.

Sure a little later than I thought, but I'm here now right?

BOOM. CIS degree underway.
